Kids Encyclopedia privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Version P1.0

Who we are

Kids Encyclopedia is made by Famama Studio (an individual developer, not a registered company). Our postal address is available on request. Contact: famamastudio@gmail.com.

The short version

In this version of the app, nothing about you or your child is sent to us. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Each child's nickname, picture choice and age band (for example 9 to 11). We never ask for a real name, birthday, school or photo.
  • Reading progress, quiz answers and badges.
  • The questions a child types into Ask me, so a grown-up can see them.
  • Mistake reports a grown-up writes.
  • Your settings, such as appearance.

This information stays in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see it. It is not used for advertising, and there are no advertising or third-party analytics tools in the app.

Ask me

Ask me answers a child's question using only the articles and answers built into the app. It does not use an AI service and does not use the internet. Questions a child types stay on the device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. If a question sounds worrying, Ask me shows a calm message that suggests telling a trusted grown-up, and in the UK it shows the Childline number.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech. The app does not record any audio.

App stores

Apple may collect information when you download or use apps, under its own privacy policy.

Deleting data

A grown-up can delete everything in the Grown-ups area: Delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Changes

If a future version starts sending any information to us, we will update this notice and ask for your agreement before anything about your child leaves the device.

Questions or complaints

Contact famamastudio@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).
